FROM THE DRAWING

  • Layout. Everything below the song windows is stage-two-decks FA.svg. The static art is the drawing's own SVG; the controls are drawn to its coordinates. Drawing (O) lays the drawing over the mockup at 50%, then as a difference, then off.
  • Two looks per control. Deck A shows one state and deck B the other, as drawn. Tap a control to change it, or Swap states (S) to flip every key, loop, PLAY, AUTO TRIM and LOCK LOWS at once.
  • Keys. MASTER TEMPO red fill / red outline; SYNC white / dark; MASTER orange gradient / orange outline (only one deck is master); HOT CUE one look; channel CUE orange gradient / outline; LOCK LOWS red outline, red fill when on.
  • Chips. EQ grey, filled from the foot; FX blue, filled from OFF at the centre ticks; TRIM green: outline and centre band when set by hand, green fill when AUTO. CUE/MST fills orange by the CUE share; PHONES orange from the foot; MASTER LEVEL yellow from the foot.
  • Transport. CUE ring orange when lit, grey when not (tap to change); PLAY green ring with ❙❙ while playing, grey ring with ▶ when stopped; loop blue when on, grey when off.
  • Faders. LEVEL on the drawing's 10 … 0 scale with its lit slot; TEMPO ±16 % on the comb, − at the top as on a CDJ; crossfader with the drawn cap.

THE POPUPS (DEXSLIDERLAB, 10 NOV TUNING)

  • Chips open on the finger. TRIM, HI, MID, LOW, FX, CUE/MST, PHONES and MASTER LEVEL open an 80 × 140 bar with the number above it, and six meter rungs (2 green, 3 yellow, 1 red, 1.5 dB apart) for the deck or the master. It closes 0.13 s after the finger lifts.
  • Relative drag. Touching changes nothing, only moving does; the bar's 140 pt covers the whole range. Move sideways for FINE ½ · ¼ · ⅒. Pulled past an end, the bar stretches.
  • Resets and detents. Double-tap TRIM for AUTO, an EQ for flat, FX for OFF, CUE/MST for 50/50. FX and CUE/MST stick gently at OFF and 50/50. LEVEL, TEMPO, PHONES and MASTER have no double-tap.
  • FX list. Hold a finger still on FX for 0.5 s: FILTER · REVERB · DELAY · GATER come up with the current one under the finger. Slide and lift to take one, or lift and tap.
  • LEVEL and TEMPO. Caps on tracks, dragged relatively. LEVEL shows its % and the master rungs above it while touched; TEMPO its % and BPM, with a gentle stop at 0.
  • Several fingers. Every finger keeps its own control, so two faders or a fader and a chip move together.
