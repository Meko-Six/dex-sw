WHAT THE DECKS ADD

  • Foot row widened. The beat-jump group is now 44 · 178 · 44 with 4 pt gaps — 274, the whole width before the channel CUE — so every length cell is 44 × 44. The channel CUE narrowed 78 → 70 and sits 4 pt right, which keeps its centre on LEVEL's at 331. Nothing else moved: same 1174 × 476 grid, same controls and states.
  • Panel. Flat matte black with a fine vertical brush, hairline edges, a raised mixer faceplate and cross-head screws. Every surface is a flat fill — the only gradients left are the lit keys, the AUTO chip and the printed textures (brush, stipple, jog grooves). Legends are silkscreen: 11 pt condensed, letter-spaced, no UI box.
  • Deck keys are caps. Bevel, engraved legend, an unlit LED in the key's colour — and the agreed elliptical gradient only when the key is lit, with a bloom in that colour.
  • Chips. Recessed caps: printed legend, segment-style value, and the fill as a flat tint with a bright level edge. Green, yellow and AUTO keep their meanings.
  • Faders. Toothed metal wells, silver caps with grip grooves, LEVEL's 10 … 0 printed on the panel outside and TEMPO's comb either side of its slot, orange 0 line.
  • Platter. The CDJ-900's dimpled aluminium ring and metal hub around the cue display, rubber face with its grooves. CUE · PLAY · loop are illuminated keys with LED rings.
  • Meter. A flat inset window, segments lit with a little bloom, CH A · MASTER · CH B and the dBFS scale printed under it.

ON THE IPAD

  • Real targets. Pointer events with capture: a drag follows the finger and never jumps; the faders and crossfader are true sliders, EQ chips drag, keys latch.
  • 44 pt minimum. The 78 × 40 deck keys carry 3 pt of invisible hit padding, so their target is 46 high. Targets (T) draws every hit area with its size; anything under 44 × 44 is red.
  • Fingers, not cursors. No hover-only states, no double-tap zoom or delayed taps (touch-action), no text selection or callout on the panel.
  • Everything is 44 or more. 47 targets measured, the smallest now the beat-jump cells at exactly 44 × 44; their strip has no top and bottom borders so the cells, not the frame, own the 44 pt. Targets (T) draws them all — nothing is red.
